# RenacidaMods

Bringing classic PC games back to life on modern hardware. "Renacida" is Spanish for "reborn".

## Projects

- **Magic Carpet 2** (via [Magic Carpet 2 HD](https://github.com/thobbsinteractive/magic-carpet-2-hd)): smooth frame rates at 4K at the game's original speed, sprites at their intended shape, a faster display mode on Windows and crash fixes. **Out now:** [RenacidaMods edition 0.1](https://github.com/RenacidaMods/magic-carpet-2-hd/releases/tag/renacidamods-0.1), a small add-on for Magic Carpet 2 HD (no game files, works with your own copy).
- **Fatal Racing** (via [ROLLER](https://github.com/FatalDecomp/ROLLER)): a mod in progress (name to be confirmed).
- More to come.

All of it is free and works with your own copy of the games.
